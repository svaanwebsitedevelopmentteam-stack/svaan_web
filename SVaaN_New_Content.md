Draft 1 for svaantech.com, written in the SVaaN Brand Guidelines V1.0 voice and the page structure of the Developer Master Specification. October 5, 2026.

## How to read this draft

- **Voice:** from the brand book. Clear, intelligent, confident, human. Plain words, main idea first, next step obvious.
- **Structure:** from the spec. Four pillars (Build, Modernize, Operate, Evolve) and the spec's page list.
- **Word choice:** the brand book allows words like meaningful, clarity and purpose when they are specific. The spec bans them on the website. I left them out, along with transformation, innovation, seamless, robust, scalable and next-generation. The brand line “Technology that moves business forward.” is kept.
- **Tags:** **\[VERIFY\]** marks a fact taken from the current site that the business must confirm. **\[NEED\]** marks information I do not have. Nothing has been invented. Section 17 lists everything I need from you.
- Every page gives a title tag, meta description and H1, then the sections in order.
- Service descriptions follow what the current site and spec already list. Delete anything SVaaN does not actually deliver.

**What we do**

- **Legacy application modernization.** Update or replace old applications without losing the rules they contain.

## 10. Approach (/approach)

**Title:** Our Approach | Understand, Decide, Build, Run, Improve | SVaaN

**Meta description:** How SVaaN works with clients: understand the problem, decide the direction, build, run and keep improving.

- **H1:** Understand. Decide. Build. Run. Improve.
- **Intro:** We do not begin with technology. We begin with understanding. Every engagement follows the same five stages, whether it is a new build, a modernization or an ongoing support service.

**Stage 1: Understand.** We learn the business problem, the users, the constraints and the current state. Examples: discovery, stakeholder conversations, a current-state review.

**Stage 2: Decide.** We choose the direction before committing to build. Examples: priorities, architecture, a roadmap, options with trade-offs.

**Stage 3: Build.** We turn the decision into working technology. Examples: design, engineering, integration, quality assurance, deployment.

**Stage 4: Run.** We keep live technology stable and useful. Examples: application support, production support, cloud management, DevOps.

**Stage 5: Improve.** We use what we learn, and what changes in the business, to keep getting better. Examples: automation, AI, enhancements, optimization.

**How we hold ourselves to it**

- **Start with the problem.** We ask why before we decide what.
- **Connect decisions to delivery.** A plan is only useful if it can be built.
- **Keep the outcome in view.** We track what changed for the business, not only what we shipped.
- **Build for change.** Systems should be easy to change when the business changes.

**Ways to work with us**

- **Project-based.** A defined piece of work with an agreed scope and timeline.
- **Dedicated team.** A team that works as part of your business over the long term.
- **Ongoing managed service.** We run and support your technology month to month.
- Staff augmentation appears on the current site. Keep it only if SVaaN wants to offer it. \[NEED\]
