# **1\. Executive Summary**

The staging site is visually strong and has a coherent modern technology-company aesthetic. The main weakness is the commercial story. The current site presents SVaaN as a broad strategy/design/engineering company, while the stronger public story is a technology partner that builds, modernizes, operates and continuously improves business-critical technology.

The new site must make a prospective founder, CEO, CTO, COO, or technology leader understand within seconds: what SVaaN does, who it helps, why it is different, what evidence exists, and what the next step should be.

| Recommended core positioningSVaaN is a technology partner for the full lifecycle of business technology — helping organizations build new software, modernize existing systems, operate critical technology and continuously improve how technology supports the business. |
| :---- |

* Mobile-first responsive behaviour at 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px and desktop widths.  
* No horizontal overflow.  
* All interactive controls keyboard accessible.  
* Visible focus states.  
* Sufficient contrast.

* Preserve the current clean, premium, modern visual language where it supports the new information architecture.  
* Reduce decorative content that does not improve comprehension or trust.

* Use subtle motion only where it helps orientation or storytelling.  
* Keep typography highly readable; break long paragraphs into shorter blocks.  
* Ensure mobile layout feels intentionally designed, not merely collapsed from desktop.

# **21\. Content / Writing Rules**

* Write like experienced business operators, not a marketing agency.  
* Prefer short, direct sentences.  
* Use concrete verbs: build, modernize, support, run, improve, integrate, automate.  
* Avoid repeated buzzwords: transformation, meaningful, clarity, purpose, innovation, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, scalable, next-generation.  
* Do not claim measurable outcomes without evidence.  
* Do not use unsupported superlatives such as “industry-leading”.  
* Explain business impact before technology.  
* Use technical depth on service pages for CTO/engineering audiences.  
* Do not make every page sound like generic corporate copy.
