### **01 — Understand**

Heading: Understand before deciding.

We clarify the business challenge, users, context, constraints, and desired progress.

Typical activities:

* Discovery  
* Stakeholder discussions  
* Problem framing  
* Current-state review  
* Requirement analysis  
* Opportunity identification

Output: A clearer understanding of the problem and what needs to change.

### **02 — Strategize**

Heading: Turn complexity into direction.

We identify priorities, opportunities, and the direction that makes sense for the situation.

Typical activities:

* Prioritization  
* Product and technology direction  
* Transformation planning  
* Solution options  
* Roadmap definition

Output: A practical direction that connects business needs with technology decisions.

### **03 — Design**

Heading: Make the solution useful before making it real.

We translate direction into experiences, products, workflows, and solution concepts.

Typical activities:

* User experience design  
* Product definition  
* Prototyping  
* Interface design  
* Solution design

Output: A clearer representation of what should be built and how it should work.

### **04 — Build**

Heading: Turn direction into working technology.

We engineer and deliver the solution with appropriate technology, quality practices, and collaboration.

Typical activities:

* Application development  
* Integration  
* Quality assurance  
* Deployment  
* Delivery management

Output: Working technology aligned with the agreed direction.

### **05 — Evolve**

Heading: Improve as the business changes.

We support, monitor, learn from, and improve technology after launch.

Typical activities:

* Application support  
* Infrastructure support  
* Production support  
* Cloud management  
* Performance improvement  
* Continuous enhancement

Output: Technology that remains useful as needs evolve.

## **7.1 POC DEVELOPMENT**

Capability: Strategy & Advisory

H1: Validate the idea before making the larger investment.

Intro:  
A proof of concept helps organizations test whether an idea, technology, or approach is viable before committing to full-scale development.

When it helps:

* You have an idea but need evidence before investing.  
* Technical feasibility is uncertain.  
* Stakeholders need something tangible to evaluate.  
* You need to test a proposed workflow or technology.  
* You want to reduce uncertainty before an MVP or larger build.

What we do:  
We translate the core question into a focused proof of concept, identify what needs to be tested, build the relevant working components, and use the results to inform the next decision.

Typical deliverables:

* Problem and hypothesis definition  
* Technical feasibility assessment  
* POC scope  
* Prototype or working proof  
* Technical findings  
* Recommendation for next steps

# **27\. EDITORIAL VOICE**

SVaaN's public content should sound:

* Intelligent  
* Clear  
* Confident  
* Human  
* Forward-thinking

## **Write like this**

"Start with the problem, then identify the technology that makes sense."

## **Avoid**

"Revolutionary, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, next-generation" unless the claim is specifically supported and approved.

## **Prefer**

* Clear business language  
* Short paragraphs  
* Specific outcomes  
* Evidence over hype  
* Business context before technical detail  
* Plain explanations of technical services

## Content rule

Do not make the technology the story when the business problem is the story.
